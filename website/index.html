Make hiring more intelligent.

ZECPATH AI is a modular recruitment platform designed to automate job-description processing, resume analysis, candidate screening, interviews and evaluation.

Explore PlatformView on GitHub ↗

One platform for the hiring lifecycle

Independent modules work together to turn unstructured recruitment data into structured candidate insights.

Resume Parsing

Extract and clean candidate information from resumes for downstream recruitment workflows.

JD Parsing

Extract roles, skills, experience and education requirements into structured JSON.

ATS Screening

Support resume-to-job matching, compatibility scoring and candidate ranking.

AI Screening

Build AI-assisted screening workflows for candidate responses and communication assessment.

AI Interviews

Support HR and technical interview workflows with structured evaluation.

Candidate Scoring

Combine recruitment signals into structured candidate evaluation and recommendations.
